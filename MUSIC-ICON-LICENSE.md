# Music launcher icon

The four beamed sixteenth notes in `source/js/music-player.js` are adapted from
[Sixteenth note run.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sixteenth_note_run.svg)
by Pko (original) and Ekips39 / 9cfilorux (vectorization), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Changes: removed staff lines, cropped the view box, slightly thickened stems for
small-screen legibility, and replaced black with `currentColor`. This adapted
icon is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.
